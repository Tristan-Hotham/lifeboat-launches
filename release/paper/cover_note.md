## Summary of the contribution (for a cover letter)

The RNLI publishes a record of every lifeboat launch, but these open records have rarely been analysed as a whole. Using all 45,673 launches from 2021 to 2025, under an analysis plan written before any outcome was computed, this paper documents the data and their problems (lost longitude signs, impossible positions, a rising "Other" category) and describes where, how far, for whom and when lifeboats go. Half of all located launches went less than 4.5 km from the station; within the same station, all-weather lifeboats went 150% further than inshore lifeboats, and commercial casualties lay further out than people in the water, while leisure craft did not. Launches to powered and sail craft were 10.6 times as frequent in summer as in winter, against 2.2 times for commercial casualties, and leisure launches rose more at weekends. Station workload was almost unrelated to coast length. The analysis is descriptive, the code is open and the released data are aggregates.
